Aero-Hopper (Creature)

Edit this page
Aero-Hopper

Type: Fauna

Habitat: Glass Plains

Diet: Herbivore

Temperament: Passive

The Aero-Hopper is a common, passive creature found throughout the Glass Plains and occasionally in the Crystal Spires biome. They are characterized by their glossy, iridescent shells and the gentle, melodic hum they emit while grazing on luminous flora.

Behavior

Aero-Hoppers travel in small herds of 3-5 individuals. They are timid and will flee from players who approach too quickly or make loud noises. When startled, their shells will flash with a bright cyan light, and they will perform a series of high-speed, low-gravity "hops" to escape danger. They are completely harmless and cannot be provoked into attacking.

Drops

When an Aero-Hopper is defeated (a morally questionable act, according to in-game lore), it may drop the following items:

  • Glossy Shell Fragment (Common) - Used in crafting decorative items.
  • Luminous Dust (Uncommon) - A key ingredient for creating light sources.
